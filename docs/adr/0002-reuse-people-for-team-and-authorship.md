# Reuse people for company team presentation and journal authorship

Person records hold an individual's name, optional public role, and optional portrait. About selects and orders core team members, while journal entries independently select and order their authors. This preserves a person's identity across both uses without making all authors core team members or requiring a portrait to credit an author.

Company collaborators remain an ordered list of names on About, as approved for NWS-2579. They do not need profiles or reusable identity yet. Project team members retain their existing meaning of contributing organizations; project responsibility is a separate relationship outside this change.

About launches with the approved introduction and collaborator list from NWS-2577 and NWS-2579. Core team records will be populated when approved names, public roles, portrait files, and display order are available. No roster or portrait mapping is inferred from internal workspace membership.
